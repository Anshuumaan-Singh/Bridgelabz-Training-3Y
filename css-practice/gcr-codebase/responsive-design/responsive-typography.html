A note on learning

Good ideas grow at their own pace.

Responsive typography lets words stay comfortable to read as the screen changes. A heading can grow on a wide display while remaining clear on a phone, and a steady line length gives each thought room to land.

At GLA University, Mathura, I am learning to combine strong foundations with small experiments. The same approach works on the web: start with a clear structure, keep the text readable, and adjust the details for the person reading it.
